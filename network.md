# Network

Your personal LinkedIn network is a reach surface Lane has never used before. Import your connections and Lane will surface the people worth reaching out to — for business development, PR, community, speaking, and influencer opportunities — as drafts you approve, the same as everything else Lane writes.

The idea: opportunities are tied to people, and the more of the right people Lane can see, the better your prospects for brand traction.

**Your data is safe with us.** The import is used for internal Lane analysis only — it's never used to message anyone automatically, and it's never exported or shared. Every resulting recommendation still requires your approval before Lane acts on it.

## Exporting your connections from LinkedIn

1. On LinkedIn, click **Me** (the menu in the top right).
2. Click **Settings & Privacy**.
3. In the left-side menu, click **Data privacy**.
4. Click **Download your data**.
5. Select the **Download larger data archive** radio button.
6. Click **Request archive**.

LinkedIn will email you a link once your archive is ready (this can take a few minutes). Download the link, unzip the archive, and find **Connections.csv** inside it.

## Importing into Lane

1. In your brand's workspace, go to **Network**.
2. Click **Upload Connections.csv** and select the file you unzipped above.
3. Review the preview — how many connections are new, already imported, or missing enough detail to classify — and check the acknowledgment box.
4. Click **Import**.

Lane picks up newly imported connections automatically on its next cycle; there's nothing further for you to do.

## Tips

- Only **Connections.csv** is supported — Lane doesn't need the rest of the LinkedIn data archive.
- Re-uploading the same file is safe: Lane only imports what's new or changed, it never duplicates.
- Your imported connections are read-only in Lane — there's no way to export them back out.
